Who we are

Act Your Age is a drama group that takes theatre out into the local community, especially to people who find it hard to get to a theatre. We perform lighter pieces in community venues, and three or four major productions a year in the Parish Rooms in Titchfield.

When we meet

Thursday afternoons at the Queen's Head, Titchfield. New faces are always welcome, on stage or behind it.

Get Involved

You don't have to be old. Just available.

Most of us are of an older age range, because Thursday afternoons suit people with a bit of time on their hands. Anyone of any age who can make it is welcome. No experience needed.
